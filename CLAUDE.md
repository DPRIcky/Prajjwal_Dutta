# Prajjwal Dutta — Portfolio Website

## What this is
A robotics/autonomy-themed personal portfolio site for Prajjwal Dutta (dual-MS, ASU — Robotics & Autonomous Systems + Manufacturing Engineering). Pure static site: hand-written HTML/CSS/JS, Three.js (r128 via cdnjs CDN) for animated 3D backgrounds. No framework, no build step — every page is self-contained and deployable as-is.

## Design system — keep every page consistent with this
- **Palette (CSS vars, defined in each page's `:root`)**: `--abyss:#060a14`, `--deep:#0a1226`, `--panel:#0e1830`, `--line:rgba(140,170,220,0.14)`, `--cyan:#4fd8e8` (primary accent), `--amber:#f5a35c` (secondary accent), `--text:#dfe8f4`, `--muted:#8a9ab5`
- **Type**: Space Grotesk (display/headings), IBM Plex Mono (labels, eyebrows, metrics, captions), Sora (body) — loaded from Google Fonts
- **Conventions**: mono uppercase eyebrows with a line rule; numbered section labels (01 /, 02 /...); metric strips (grid of big cyan numbers + tiny mono labels); solid, lit 3D machines on every page (Oct 2026: the old wireframe backgrounds were all replaced, see below); `.reveal` IntersectionObserver scroll animations; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected everywhere; mobile breakpoint at 680–720px reduces 3D object counts/scales
- **3D backgrounds are thematic per page**: index.html (Oct 2026 rewrite) is a **scroll-driven stage tour of solid PBR robots**, one stage per section in a single long world (stages 46 units apart on x), each a lit plinth with an articulated machine built from three.js primitives (no model files, still works from file://). Order (stages anchor to any element, not only sections; a section with no stage keeps the previous machine): hero six-axis arm doing real IK pick and place (elbow-up, tool held vertical, cube rides the solved tool point), **holds through About** / research AUV gyre with a live delta-BFS tree (smallest-ID root) / work SCARA moving wafers cassette to edge-belt conveyor (fork stays parallel to the belts so it drops into the gap), over the wafer cell and CLF-CBF-QP cards / **blimp anchored at `#work > .project:nth-of-type(3)`, the Tube-MPC card** (requested by Prajjwal, Oct 2026), playing the Defend the Republic catch-and-score game per his description: **blunt round end is the nose**, envelope tapers to a cone at the finned tail; the two ducted **thrusters are on struts off the envelope's flanks, not on the gondola**; a hoop-and-bag **catching net** hangs under the gondola; goals are a **circle, triangle and square** on posts. State machine in `buildBlimp`: seek (steer the net onto the drifting balloon) / carry (line up square to the next goal, net level with its centre) / shoot (balloon through the frame, goal flashes, score++) / respawn. A holo panel prints the state, target goal and score / projects air-ground team: mecanum rover strafing a square with 45 deg rollers + LiDAR, with the quadrotor (`makeQuad`/`flyQuad`) circling above it on a lag, plus the **foldable bat-wing robot** (`buildBatRig`, requested Oct 2026) flying a banked loop beneath the quadrotor: one central servo, counter-rotating meshed gear pair, humerus + folding elbow + three finger spars per wing with a translucent membrane, on that project's real gait (1.15 Hz, 0.62 rad, fold on the upstroke). Prajjwal asked for it to fly rather than sit on a bench stand, even though the real prototype was bench-tested; keep the page copy honest about that. **Holds through Skills** / contact TurtleBot 4 beside its dock. World layout zig-zags (`STEP` 34): each stage is one step deeper in -z and one step toward its own side in x, so the camera pans toward the incoming robot and it enters from the side it will sit on; do not go back to a straight +x line (everything then flies in from the right). Rig rules: each machine **holds for its whole section** and the camera flies on a crane arc only in a window of +/-0.3 viewport around each section boundary (`measure()` computes `t0/t1` per stage; never go back to centre-to-centre interpolation, long sections strand the camera between stages). **Layout lane:** from 1100px wide every `section` is docked left at `width:58vw; margin-left:6vw` (hero copy capped the same, hero h1 drops to 5.2vw so it stays three lines) so cards never cover the robots; the auto-fit grids reflow to 2 columns on their own. `#work` and `#research` are narrower still (`min(760px,44vw)`, metric strips 2x2 until 1700px) because their cards carry big image banners. `stageCam()` mirrors the breakpoint (`docked()`); `measure()` reads each stage's enclosing section box and stores the free lane beside it (`laneC` centre in NDC, `laneW` width), the robot is centred in that lane and the camera pulls back to fit it (x1.38, scaled by lane width, times an optional per-stage `cam.fit`: fleet 1.25, rover team 1.22). Below 1100px the per-stage `sh` offsets apply. The quadrotor's tilt comes from low-passed finite-difference velocity/acceleration and is itself eased (`flyQuad`); raw per-frame differencing made it jitter. Keep the CSS breakpoint and `docked()` in sync. **Robots alternate sides**: a section with `class="dock-r"` (research, projects, skills) docks its copy right and its robot frames the left lane; `s.side` is read from the anchor's enclosing section (`el.closest('section')`) (single source of truth, so to swap a stage's side just move the class). Left stages mirror the camera orbit (`az*side`) so the machine faces into the page. Two fixed scrims (`.scrim-l`/`.scrim-r`) crossfade in `applyMood()` with the camera's side, and `#stageTag` moves to its robot's side (`.left`). A left scrim on `.vignette` keeps copy legible. Sky dome and fog are in **display space** (raw hex, no `convertSRGBToLinear`, no tone map on the dome) because r128 mixes fog in after tone mapping; material colours go through `lin()`. **Stage dressing** (Oct 2026, `dressX(root, api)` functions hooked in via `dress:` in DEFS, each builder returns the live state it needs): arm = stack light (amber flashes while moving), parts bin, hazard band, holo panel printing the **live IK joint angles**; research = **under-ice** (requested: it matches the under-ice exploration work, kelp was removed) with a displaced sea-ice ceiling at y 6, faceted keels kept above the fleet's depth band (their displacement hashes vertex *position*, not index, because the faces are unwelded), brash ice, light through leads, bubbles; SCARA = cleanroom FFU ceiling + laminar downflow particles + FOUP pods + holo panel of the real cycle (slot, phase, wafers placed); blimp = faint clouds, stars, ground station (front left, clear of the goals) with a dashed live uplink, catch-and-score holo panel; rover team = warehouse racks, pallet, skylight shafts with dust, LiDAR sparks only where the fan geometrically crosses a post; TurtleBot = signal ripples, fireflies, plants. Shared FX toolkit: `dotTex` glow sprite (all particles use it), `swarm()`, `shaft()`, `holo()` canvas panels that face the camera and redraw at about 7 Hz. Holo text is visible copy, so the no-dash rule applies to it. Stage HUD `#stageTag` bottom right (hidden on phones and over the footer). Only stages within 1.3 of the camera are visible/simulated. Reduced motion renders a still of the nearest stage and cuts on scroll. Project pages (Oct 2026) use the same look: see **Solid stages on project pages** below. Every new project page should get its own concept-relevant scene.

## Light / dark theme (Oct 2026, every page: index.html and all 21 project pages)
- `<html data-theme="dark|light">` is set by a tiny inline script in `<head>` **before first paint**. **Dark is the default on load** (Prajjwal's call; the OS preference is deliberately ignored); only a saved `localStorage.theme` of `light` switches it. The key is site-wide, so the choice follows the visitor between pages. Toggle is `#themeBtn` (sun/moon SVG) inside `.nav-right`, deliberately **outside `.nav-links`** so it survives the phone breakpoint. It dispatches a `themechange` event.
- CSS: never write raw rgba for palette colours; use the channel tokens `rgba(var(--ink-rgb),a)` (overlays, scrims, nav), `--card-rgb`/`--card2-rgb` (card fills), `--cyan-rgb`, `--amber-rgb`, `--line-rgb`, `--shadow-rgb`. Light values live in `:root[data-theme="light"]` and are **warm lamplight, never white** (Prajjwal rejected the first white version as "LED lights flashing" and asked for "bed side table warm"): --abyss #e6d8bc, --deep #eee2c9, --panel #f5ecd9, --text #2e2416, --muted #63523a, accents **--cyan #0a6a74 / --amber #974607** (5.6:1 or better on the cards). The light vignette darkens the corners warm (rgba(96,62,18,0.24)) instead of brightening them. **Card covers in light mode** use a **redrawn light set** in `assets/ai/light/` (same filenames): teal and amber ink on cream, generated from the dark originals by `tools/relight_covers.py` (local background estimate, ink amount from how far a pixel rises above it, ink colour picked by warmth: cyan to teal #0a6470, amber to #b85c12, neutral to warm graphite). A CSS invert filter was tried first and rejected by Prajjwal as not looking good. The theme script swaps each `.pthumb img`/`.gthumb img` src between the two sets; covers are lazy-loaded, so a light-mode visitor only downloads the light set. **When a new card cover is added, run `python tools/relight_covers.py assets/ai assets/ai/light <name>`** to make its twin. Exceptions that stay dark on purpose: the overlays drawn on the dark artwork tiles (`.pthumb::after`, `.award::after` text-shadow) and the video lightbox, which re-declares the dark token set on `.lb`. The light nav is near-opaque because its blur smeared the dark tiles scrolling beneath it.
- 3D scene `applyTheme(light)`: per-stage `skyL` palettes in golden lamp tones (display space, like `sky`; blimp is a sunset dusk, under-ice a warmed teal), honey floor + a second `gridL`, amber key light 0xffd59a, hemi sky pulled 65% toward 0xffd394, exposure 0.78; particles registered in `PARTS` switch additive to normal blending at 0.42x tint (additive glow vanishes on a bright sky); light shafts in `SHAFTS` drop to 0.3x; holo panels in `HOLOS` switch blending and redraw from the `HC()` palette (dark text on frosted glass). Anything new that glows must register in one of these.
